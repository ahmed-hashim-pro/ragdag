Warranty and Service Policy

This policy covers Acme Robotics hardware purchased directly from Acme or an authorised reseller.

Standard Warranty

Every Meridian-3 robot carries a 24-month limited warranty from the date of commissioning. Dockyard-2 charging stations carry a 36-month warranty.

The battery pack is warranted to retain at least 80% of rated capacity for 2,000 full charge cycles or 24 months, whichever comes first.

Exclusions

  • Drive-train damage from operating above the 150 kg absolute maximum payload.
  • Water ingress beyond the IP54 rating, including pressure washing.
  • Damage caused by third-party docking hardware or unapproved firmware.

Service Levels

Customers on the Standard plan receive next-business-day remote support. Customers on the Premier plan receive a 4-hour on-site response for robots classified as fleet-critical, and a loaner robot within 48 hours when a repair exceeds 3 days.

Making a Claim

Open a ticket with the robot serial number (format MRD3-YYYY-NNNNN) and the diagnostic bundle exported from the fleet console. Claims must be raised within 30 days of the fault being observed.
